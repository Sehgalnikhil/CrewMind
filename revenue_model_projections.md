# CrewMind Revenue Model & Financial Projections

Based on the strategic positioning of CrewMind as an **Autonomous AI Executive Operating System** for consulting firms, legal teams, and corporate strategy departments, we have modeled a high-margin, B2B SaaS revenue structure explicitly around our established pricing catalog in Indian Rupees (INR).

---

## 💰 1. Official Pricing Strategy

CrewMind uses a **Tiered SaaS Pricing Model** bundled with specific feature gates (like the War Room and Knowledge Graph) and hard usage limits, encouraging seamless upsells as organizations scale. 

### Core Subscription Plans
1. **Starter (Free)**
   - *Target:* Solo explorers, hobbyists.
   - *Limits:* 1 user, 1 Workspace, 500 AI credits, 1 GB storage.
   - *Features:* Basic chat, memory.
2. **Founder (₹2,999 / month or ₹23,990 / year)**
   - *Target:* Boutique firms, startups.
   - *Limits:* 5 users, 2 Workspaces, 2,500 AI credits, 10 GB storage.
   - *Features:* Adds Reports and Knowledge Graph.
3. **Growth (₹9,999 / month or ₹79,990 / year)**
   - *Target:* Medium consulting agencies, legal teams.
   - *Limits:* 25 users, 5 Workspaces, 10,000 AI credits, 50 GB storage.
   - *Features:* Unlocks the core OS value: **War Room, Digital Twin, and Simulator.**
4. **Business (₹24,999 / month or ₹199,990 / year)**
   - *Target:* Large enterprise teams.
   - *Limits:* 100 users, 20 Workspaces, 50,000 AI credits, 250 GB storage.
   - *Features:* Adds Advanced RBAC, API Access, and Audit Logs.
5. **Enterprise (Custom Pricing)**
   - *Target:* Massive corporations needing SSO, massive storage (10 TB+), and millions of AI credits.

### Add-on Marketplace (Upsells)
We utilize a modular add-on system to generate expansion revenue without forcing users to upgrade tiers prematurely:
- **AI Credit Top-ups:** ₹499 (1k credits) to ₹5,999 (20k credits).
- **Integrations:** GitHub, Google Workspace, and Slack (₹299/mo each).
- **Premium Modules:** Custom AI Models (₹999/mo) and Voice Executive (₹499/mo).

---

## 📈 2. Real-World Performance & Adoption (The "Flywheel")

In the real world, this pricing performs exceptionally well because it severely undercuts human labor costs. 
For a 25-person consulting firm, paying **₹9,999/month (Growth Plan)** equates to just **₹400 per user/month**. 
If the **War Room** and **Digital Twin** successfully automate the initial research and synthesis phase for even one client pitch, it has saved the firm tens of thousands of rupees in billable junior analyst hours.

The real-world adoption motion is **Bottom-Up**:
1. An analyst discovers CrewMind and uses the **Free Starter** plan to speed up their own workflow.
2. They produce superior reports in half the time, impressing partners.
3. The firm upgrades to the **Founder (₹2,999)** plan to allow a small 5-person team to collaborate on the Knowledge Graph.
4. As they realize the value of the **War Room** multi-agent pipeline, they quickly hit the 5-user limit and upgrade to **Growth (₹9,999)**.
5. Eventually, IT mandates compliance, pushing them to **Business (₹24,999)** for RBAC and Audit Logs.
